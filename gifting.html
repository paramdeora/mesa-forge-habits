How it works

Gifting made simple.

01

Choose your set

Select from our curated gift sets or build your own by choosing any combination of candles from the full collection.

02

Write your note

Add a personal message at checkout. We'll print it on a handmade paper note card and place it inside the box.

03

We wrap it

Every gift order is wrapped in tissue, placed in our matte gift box, and sealed. Always. No extra fee, no separate selection needed.

04

Delivered directly

Ship to yourself or directly to the recipient. Specify the address at checkout. No prices on the packaging. Ever.
