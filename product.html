Dusk Vetiver opens with a quick flash of black pepper and the dry warmth of smoked cedar — a reference to wood burning somewhere nearby. As it settles, vetiver takes over: earthy, complex, a little wild. Fig adds softness without sweetness. The base is deep and settled: sandalwood, labdanum, and a musk that stays close to the skin.

This is an evening candle. Light it as the light changes.

Each Lantern candle is made with a proprietary blend of coconut wax and soy wax — cleaner burning than paraffin, with a longer scent-throw and a more even melt pool. The fragrance oils used are of perfumery grade: the same quality used by fine fragrance houses. The frosted glass vessel is reusable and recyclable. The cotton-core wick is lead-free.

  • On first use, burn until the entire surface is liquid — this prevents tunnelling. This may take 2–3 hours.
  • Trim the wick to 5mm before each burn. A long wick causes smoking and uneven burning.
  • Do not burn for more than 4 hours at a time.
  • Keep away from drafts, vents, and flammable surfaces.
  • Never leave a burning candle unattended.
  • Stop burning when 1cm of wax remains.
