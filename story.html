Our Story

Made for the
quality of a room's light.

"We didn't set out to make candles. We set out to make the kind of atmosphere that makes a room feel like it belongs to you."

— Arya Menon, Founder

01

It began with a
problem of atmosphere.

Lantern started in 2021, in a small apartment in Bengaluru where the fragrance options were either too sweet, too synthetic, or too ordinary. We had lived with premium fragrance — fine perfume, incense from temple towns — and we couldn't understand why home scent always felt like an afterthought.

We began experimenting. Wax blends, wick types, fragrance oil ratios, pouring temperatures. We tested 400 combinations before we found one we were proud of. That first formula became Dusk Vetiver.

02

Fragrance as a
serious discipline.

We source fragrance oils from perfumery-grade suppliers — the same houses that supply to Maison Margiela and Jo Malone. We do not use fragrance compounds designed for mass-market candles. The price difference is significant. We absorb it.

Our wax is a proprietary blend of coconut and soy — cleaner burning than paraffin, with a lower melting point that allows fragrance to release gradually rather than in a single overwhelming burst.

Every candle is poured at a precise temperature, by hand, in our studio. Batch size is limited by what we can physically pour in a day.

03

An Indian luxury
that doesn't apologise for itself.

Luxury home fragrance in India has long looked to Paris for permission. We don't. Our references are the scent of rain on red earth in Kerala. Cardamom from a Coorg morning. Old sandalwood from a Mysuru market. Incense drifting through a Benaras corridor.

These are world-class references. We treat them as such. Lantern is a luxury brand that happens to be Indian — not an Indian brand trying to feel luxurious.

What we
stand for.

Four principles that govern every decision we make — from the ingredients we source to the way we ship.

◈

No shortcuts

Premium ingredients, small batches, hands that pour. We will never optimise the craft out of the candle to reduce cost.

◉

Clean burning

Natural wax, cotton wicks, no paraffin, no synthetic dyes. What goes into the air in your home should be something you'd choose.

◎

Honest pricing

We price for quality, not for brand inflation. You pay for what's inside the vessel. No margin hidden in the logo.

◇

Considered design

Packaging that earns its place on a shelf. Nothing excessive, nothing disposable. The vessel outlives the candle.

How we make it

From raw material
to finished candle.

1

Sourcing

Fragrance oils sourced from perfumery-grade suppliers. Wax imported from certified natural farms.

2

Blending

Each fragrance is blended in-house and allowed to rest for 48 hours before pouring begins.

3

Pouring

Wax is heated to a precise temperature and poured by hand into cured, pre-wicked vessels.

4

Curing

Candles cure for a minimum of 72 hours before inspection. This ensures an even melt pool and full scent throw.

5

Dispatch

Each candle is inspected, labelled, and packed in tissue before shipping — directly from our studio.

A note from the founder

I started Lantern because I couldn't find a candle I actually wanted to keep in my home. Everything was too sweet, too perfumed, too obviously trying to communicate luxury through packaging rather than substance.

I spent two years learning about fragrance before I felt confident enough to sell anything. I studied how fine perfume houses think about scent construction — top, heart, base, dry-down, diffusion. I applied that thinking to candles. The results were different enough from anything else in the market that I felt we had something worth sharing.

Lantern is still small. Every candle is still poured in our studio. I like it this way. There is something about scale that changes the quality of attention. We will grow carefully.

Arya Arya Menon · Founder & Creative Director
