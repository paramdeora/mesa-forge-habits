On craft

Every candle begins
with a moment of quiet.

We don't make candles for fragrance. We make them for atmosphere — the specific quality of light and scent that changes the feeling of a room, a morning, an evening between friends.

Each fragrance is developed over months. Each pour is done by hand. Nothing is rushed.

Read Our Story

Our approach

Fragrance as
a second architecture.

We believe a scent should do what a room does — hold you, orient you, make you feel at home. Every note in every Lantern candle is chosen not for impact, but for character.

01

Raw Materials

We source from perfumery-grade suppliers who supply to fine fragrance houses. No synthetic shortcuts.

02

Wax Blend

Our proprietary coconut-soy blend burns cooler and cleaner than paraffin, releasing fragrance gradually rather than all at once.

03

The Pour

Temperature, timing, and silence. Each candle is poured at a specific moment in the cooling process to ensure even fragrance distribution.
